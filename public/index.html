What it does

Hand-drawn UML

Classes, interfaces, enums, use cases and every UML link, drawn in an Excalidraw style. Double-click to edit in place.

Live design checks

About 50 rules for SOLID, encapsulation, coupling and UML 2.5. Mistakes get a badge and a short explanation right on the canvas.

Organize big diagrams

Group classes into packages, modules, components, containers or plugins, then collapse them for an overview.

Share a link

Publish a read-only link that expires after an hour, a day or a week. No account needed.

Sketch and annotate

Pen, highlighter, shapes, arrows and a laser pointer for presenting, with a style panel for colors and widths.

Optional AI help

Draft classes from a problem description and get a short review. Everything else works without AI.
